# Verification, consent and cultural safety protocol

## 1. Consent comes before capture

Before recording a person, explain in a language they understand:
- what Living Skills Lineage is;
- what will be recorded;
- where it may appear;
- whether it may be translated;
- whether learning or commercial contact will be offered;
- how they can restrict or withdraw material.

Consent is granular. Agreement to an interview does not imply agreement to publish every technique.

## 2. Preserve the original voice

Keep the original recording and language. Transcripts and translations are linked versions, not replacements. Machine translation may create a draft, but culturally important material should be reviewed by a fluent human before publication.

## 3. Separate testimony from verified fact

A practitioner may say, “Only our family still does this.” Preserve that testimony as testimony. The platform must not rewrite it as “the last family in the world” without corroborating evidence.

## 4. High-consequence claims require review

The following require explicit evidence:
- “last practitioner” / “only N practitioners remain”;
- endangered or critically endangered status;
- lineage/generation counts;
- origin or ownership claims;
- exclusive community claims;
- historical dates;
- certifications or master titles.

Each published claim shows, where appropriate, source type and last verified date.

## 5. Preservation status methodology

Assess transmission, not popularity alone:
- known active practitioners;
- age distribution where safely available;
- active apprentices;
- completeness of transmission;
- frequency of practice;
- access to materials/tools/ecology;
- community assessment;
- quality and recency of evidence.

When evidence is weak, use **insufficient evidence**.

## 6. Cultural permissions

Every documented item receives an access policy:
- Public
- Community-only
- Learning permitted
- Commercial use restricted
- Sacred/private
- Documentation prohibited

The strictest applicable community/practitioner restriction wins.

## 7. Corrections and withdrawal

Profiles need a visible correction path. Consent withdrawal triggers editorial review and removal/restriction of affected public media as appropriate. Backups and audit records are handled under a documented retention policy.

## 8. No extraction

The project should never pressure a practitioner to reveal guarded knowledge merely to make a page “complete.” A meaningful blank is better than violating a boundary.
